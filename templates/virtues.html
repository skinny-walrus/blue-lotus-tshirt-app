Qualities of an open heart

Four ways to meet the world.

In Buddhist practice, these four qualities are known as the brahmavihāras, or Four Immeasurables. They invite us to extend goodwill and care beyond the people closest to us. They are a starting point for this collection, within the wider Buddhist tradition.

Learn more: The Four Sublime States — Nyanaponika Thera. Daily practices on this page are suggested reflections, not quotations.

Bhante Sujatha · Love In Action

A kind intention.

For Bhante Sujatha, humanitarian service brings meditation into everyday life. His Love In Action work encourages us to give what we can and respond when there is an opportunity to help.

This new collection carries forward Blue Lotus Temple’s connection between loving-kindness and practical service. Profits will help support Bhante’s humanitarian work.
